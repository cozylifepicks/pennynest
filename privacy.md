PennyNest respects your privacy. This site is a simple content blog — we don't
ask you to create an account or hand over personal details to read our guides.

**Analytics.** We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which
articles are helpful (for example, page views). This data is aggregated and not
used to identify you personally.

**Affiliate links.** Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click
one and make a purchase, the retailer (such as Amazon or a course platform) may
set its own cookies and share a small commission with us, at no extra cost to
you. See our [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/) for details.

**Third parties.** Links out to other websites are governed by those sites'
own privacy policies, not ours.

If you have any questions about privacy on PennyNest, feel free to reach out.
